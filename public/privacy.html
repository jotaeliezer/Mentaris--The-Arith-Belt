Privacy Policy for Mentaris - The Arith Belt

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Mentaris - The Arith Belt is a math practice game made for kids and students. We built it to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

When a player finishes a mission, the game may send the following to our online leaderboard: a pilot name that the game generates at random (for example, "Nova Falcon 42"), the score, the game mode, and the date and time. Players cannot type in their own name, so the leaderboard does not contain real names.

What stays on your device

Campaign progress, best scores, and settings are saved in your browser or on your computer. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the game deletes them.

What we don't do

We do not ask for names, email addresses, photos, locations, or accounts. We do not show ads. We do not use tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell or share information with anyone.

Service providers

The game is hosted on Vercel, and leaderboard scores are stored with Supabase. Like any website, these services may briefly record technical details such as IP addresses to keep the game running and secure. They do not use this information to identify players, and they only process it on our behalf.

Children's privacy

Mentaris is designed for children. Because we don't collect personal information from players, children can play without creating an account or sharing who they are. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and design the game to meet the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Deleting leaderboard entries

A parent, guardian, or teacher can ask us to remove any leaderboard entry. Email us with the pilot name and roughly when the game was played, and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it collects (for example, when classroom features are added), we will update this policy before the change goes live and change the date at the top.
